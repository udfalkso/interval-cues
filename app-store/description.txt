Run your intervals. Keep your rhythm.

Interval Cues gives you clear spoken prompts for every stage of your workout, so you can spend less time checking your phone and more time moving. Build your session, start your favorite music or podcast, and let the cues guide each change of pace.

YOUR WORKOUT, YOUR WAY
Create and save workouts with warm-ups, fast efforts, recovery intervals, walks, cool-downs, and custom steps. Combine steps into repeat blocks to build the session you want without entering every interval by hand. Duplicate a favorite workout and adjust it for your next run.

CLEAR CUES, HANDS-FREE
Hear each phase change and a completion announcement. Turn on an optional 30-second warning for a heads-up before the next step. On iPhone, workout timing and spoken cues continue with the screen locked or another app open.

BRING YOUR OWN SOUNDTRACK
Choose how cues interact with other audio: pause compatible spoken-audio playback, lower background audio, or speak over it. Podcast pausing depends on the playback app. Choose an installed voice and adjust the speaking speed to suit you.

STAY IN CONTROL
A large countdown shows the current interval, with the next phase and total workout time close at hand. Pause, resume, skip a phase, or stop whenever you need to.

SIMPLE FROM THE FIRST RUN
Start with the included Cardio Intervals workout or build your own. No account is required. Your saved workouts and settings stay on your device.

Whether you are alternating runs and walks or repeating hard efforts and recovery, Interval Cues helps you follow the workout you planned.
