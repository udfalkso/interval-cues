No account, sign-in, subscription, or demo credentials are required. The app includes a Cardio Intervals workout. You can also create a short workout to test phase changes quickly.

To test audio cues, start music or a podcast in another app, then start a workout. Settings offers Pause, Lower, and Over audio modes; podcast pausing depends on the other app identifying its audio as spoken content. Lock the iPhone or switch to another app to test background cues.

The audio background mode supports the native workout timer and spoken interval announcements during an active workout. It is not used to stream content. A silent audio loop maintains the audio session between announcements. Stop the workout to end the session.

Saved workouts and preferences are stored locally. The app does not record audio, track location, or integrate with HealthKit.
