# Interval Cues Support

Interval Cues helps you follow custom interval workouts with spoken prompts.

For help, questions, or bug reports, email [udi@breasy.com](mailto:udi@breasy.com). Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

## Getting started

Open the included Cardio Intervals workout or create your own. Add timed steps for warm-up, fast efforts, recovery, walking, or cool-down. Use repeat blocks for repeated intervals. Start your workout to hear phase changes and, if enabled, a 30-second warning.

## Music and podcasts

Settings offers three cue modes: Pause, Lower, and Over. Pause can interrupt compatible podcast or other spoken-audio apps; behavior depends on the playback app. Lower reduces other audio during announcements. Over plays announcements alongside other audio.

Choose an installed voice and speaking speed in Settings. Additional voices can be downloaded through iOS accessibility settings.

## Background workouts

On iPhone, an active workout can continue announcing intervals with the screen locked or another app open. Calls, Siri, and other system interruptions can temporarily interrupt audio. Force-quitting the app stops its operation. Test your setup before relying on it for a full workout.

## Saved workouts

Workouts and settings are stored on your device. There is no account or cloud synchronization. Removing the app removes its locally stored data.
