# Interval Cues Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Interval Cues is provided by Udi Falkson. This policy describes the app's handling of information.

## App data

Interval Cues does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, an account system, or a developer-operated server.

Your workouts, custom step labels, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Workout timing and speech announcements are processed on your device using system audio and speech services. The app does not request microphone, location, contacts, or HealthKit access.

The app's local data may be included in device backups managed by Apple according to your device settings. Apple manages those services under its own privacy policy. The developer cannot access those backups.

## Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives the email address and information you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues. It is not sold or used for advertising. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the address below.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete saved workouts in the app. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data from the device. Backup copies are governed by your backup settings.

## Third-party services

The App Store, device operating system, and any music or podcast app you use have their own privacy policies. This policy covers Interval Cues.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

For privacy questions, contact [udi@breasy.com](mailto:udi@breasy.com).
